Third-party protocol material in Radio.TV.Control:

- tvremote-core/src/main/proto/polo.proto follows the Google TV pairing protocol field definitions distributed by tronikos/androidtvremote2 under Apache-2.0.
- tvremote-core/src/main/proto/remotemessage.proto is a small subset of Android TV Remote v2 message field definitions, originally published by louis49/androidtv-remote under MIT and also distributed by tronikos/androidtvremote2 under Apache-2.0.
- License texts are retained in licenses/APACHE-2.0.txt and licenses/ANDROIDTV-REMOTE-MIT.txt.
- No implementation source from GT-TAHAKOM or Atharok/BtRemote is copied into this project; those projects are architecture references only.
